Our Journey

How We Got Here

2020 — The Beginning

Started baking koeke and beskuit from a home kitchen for friends and family.

2022 — First Market

Began selling at local markets every weekend — sold out most Saturdays.

2024 — Cafe Opens

Opened our permanent location in Madabani, Venda.

Today

Serving fresh treats daily and growing our family of regulars.
